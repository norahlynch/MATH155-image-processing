
# Output Images and Results

## 1. 3x3 Averaging Filter

The image after applying a uniform 3x3
spatial averaging kernel.

Each output pixel is calculated from the
average intensity of its local neighborhood.

The result demonstrates moderate image smoothing.

## 2. 9x9 Averaging Filter

The image after applying a uniform 9x9
spatial averaging kernel.

The larger neighborhood produces stronger
smoothing and greater suppression of fine details.


<img width="246" height="260" alt="filtered_image_m=9" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8f8edaa4-a0d0-40c1-9795-1db8c4034415" />

## Results

Increasing the averaging kernel size produces
greater spatial smoothing at the expense of
high-frequency image information.
