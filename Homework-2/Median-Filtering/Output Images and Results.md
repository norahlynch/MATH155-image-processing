
# Output Images and Results

## 1. Median-Filtered Image

The image after applying a 3x3 median filter.

Each pixel is replaced by the median intensity
of its local neighborhood, suppressing isolated
intensity outliers.

## 2. Original and Filtered Image Comparison

A comparison of the original grayscale image
and the median-filtered result.

## Results

Median filtering reduces impulse noise while
preserving many structural boundaries more
effectively than conventional averaging.
